# UI Review Checklist
Apple Human Interface Guidelines × NeXTSTEP / OPENSTEP / GNUstep Inspired Product

## 1. Intent
- Is the purpose of the screen immediately clear?
- Does the screen support a real task efficiently?
- Does it feel professional, calm, and precise?
- Does it avoid looking like a retro replica?

## 2. Apple HIG Compliance
- Are platform conventions respected?
- Are standard Apple interaction patterns used?
- Are controls understandable and familiar?
- Are dialogs, sheets, menus, and settings appropriate?
- Is the hierarchy readable?
- Is accessibility preserved?

## 3. NeXT / OPENSTEP / GNUstep Influence
- Is the composition pane-based and structured?
- Does the layout feel workstation-like?
- Are inspectors used appropriately?
- Is the toolbar disciplined?
- Is the atmosphere restrained and technical?
- Is the influence expressed through structure rather than legacy imitation?

## 4. Layout
- Is the main task area dominant?
- Are sidebar and inspector roles clear?
- Is the layout segmented logically?
- Is spacing efficient and consistent?
- Is information density appropriate for desktop use?
- Is the design compact without being cramped?

## 5. Visual Design
- Is the base palette restrained and neutral?
- Is accent color limited and meaningful?
- Are visual effects subtle?
- Are separators and grouping cues clear?
- Is noise minimized?
- Is decorative styling avoided?

## 6. Components
- Are native controls used where possible?
- Are custom controls justified?
- Are tables, lists, and forms readable?
- Are selection and focus states clear?
- Are disabled, warning, and destructive states understandable?
- Do inspector panels feel functional?

## 7. Typography
- Is system typography used appropriately?
- Is hierarchy clear?
- Is text readable at working distance?
- Are labels concise and unambiguous?
- Is the text density appropriate for productivity work?
- Are decorative fonts avoided?

## 8. Interaction
- Are common actions easy to locate?
- Is keyboard efficiency considered?
- Are repeated tasks low-friction?
- Are confirmations used appropriately?
- Are errors actionable and concise?
- Does the screen support sustained use?

## 9. Accessibility
- Is contrast sufficient?
- Is color not the only carrier of meaning?
- Are focus states visible?
- Are controls targetable?
- Are labels meaningful?
- Is readability preserved?

## 10. Consistency
- Does this match the rest of the product?
- Are repeated patterns implemented consistently?
- Are naming and icon use consistent?
- Are toolbar patterns consistent?
- Are sidebar and inspector behaviors consistent?
- Does this feel like one coherent system?

## 11. Anti-Pattern Rejection
Reject or revise if the screen:
- looks nostalgic rather than timeless
- copies old UI details literally
- feels like generic SaaS
- overuses cards
- overuses rounded corners
- overuses gradients, shadows, or glass
- wastes desktop space
- hides key actions
- replaces native behavior unnecessarily
- prioritizes styling over usability

## 12. Approval Rule
A screen passes only if:
- Apple usability is preserved
- accessibility is preserved
- the layout supports real work
- the visual tone is restrained and professional
- NeXT / OPENSTEP / GNUstep influence appears in composition and atmosphere, not outdated behavior
